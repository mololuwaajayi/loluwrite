Founder Newsletter Edition Sample
Nobody Told Me Building a Startup Would Feel Like This
Written in the voice of an African tech founder

Six months ago, I thought the hard part was the idea. I had a deck, a vision, and the kind of energy that makes people nod along in meetings. What I did not have was a single user.

Here is what they do not put in the startup playbooks: the silence after launch is louder than any noise you have ever heard. Your product is live. The notification sound you imagined playing never comes.

But this week something changed. Not the metrics, those are still humble. What changed was me. I stopped waiting for the product to prove itself and started talking to the people I built it for. Twelve calls. Twelve different versions of the same honest feedback. And suddenly I understood what we were actually building.

Your first users will not come to you. You go to them. Every time.

Demonstration sample showing voice adaptation for a founder client. Available for retainer ghostwriting.
LinkedIn Thought Leadership Post Sample
The best career advice I got was from someone who had already failed
Written in the voice of a senior professional

Three years into my career, I met a man who had built three companies. Two of them had failed spectacularly. He was the most confident person in the room.

I asked him how. He said: "I stopped treating failure like a verdict. It is just data."

That reframe changed how I approach every project. I no longer ask "What if this doesn't work?" I ask "What will I learn when this doesn't go exactly as planned?"

There is a version of ambition that is fragile. And then there is the kind built on genuine curiosity. The second kind is what builds lasting careers. And it is a choice you make before results arrive, not after.

Demonstration sample for LinkedIn ghostwriting. I adapt fully to your voice, tone, and industry.
